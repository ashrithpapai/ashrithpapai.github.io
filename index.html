01 · Patient outreach

Patient outreach automation at health-system scale

Schedulers worked referral queues line by line, an outside vendor ran pre-registration calls, and quality teams had no automated way to reach patients overdue for diabetes, pediatric or screening care. Every missed contact was a missed appointment, a missed quality measure and lost revenue.

~50,000
manual outreach attempts a year taken off staff (projected)

What I architected and delivered

  • Referral work-queue automation, 8 queues across 2 service lines. Data extensions, SQL automations and Journey Builder flows that turn every referral order into a timed email and SMS sequence, with reason codes written back cleanly to the patient record.
  • In-house pre-registration program that replaced an outside vendor. Daily Epic EHR file over SFTP, header-less import, SQL segmentation for consented patients with appointments in the next 30 days, multi-step email and SMS, and a results file back to the EHR. Solved UTF-16 to UTF-8 encoding, layout changes, age-gated AMPscript personalization, weekend suppression and reminder spacing.
  • Diabetes (HbA1c) quality-gap journey, built from zero, iterated with the quality team, with encounter logging and holiday-aware due dates.
  • Value-based care enhancements: 90- and 180-day pediatric overdue tiers, hospice exclusions across every value-based journey, and high-risk cervical-screening annual-letter logic.
  • Gynecology patient-education journey across 16 visit types in a rolling 30-day window — and when the planned microsite slipped, I built the landing page in SFMC to hold the launch date.

Business impact

  • Automated ~200 daily queue contacts, building on an earlier phase that cut referral contact effort by nearly 50%.
  • Outside pre-registration vendor replaced by a closed-loop program the team fully controls.
  • Journeys now cover diabetes, pediatric and adult preventive, Medicare wellness, colorectal and breast screening, and specialty education.
  • Clinical safety built in: hospice patients excluded system-wide, scheduled patients no longer told to schedule, weekend and holiday due dates back-filled. Every journey signed off by its clinical owner.

04 · Compliance & governance

Compliance, security and platform health

In one year the platform faced a carrier audit flagging 8 SMS short codes, a mandatory 180-day OAuth rotation with a hard deadline, millions of dead contacts and daylight-saving shifts that could knock every send time off by an hour.

8 of 8
short codes passed carrier re-audit in under 4 weeks

What I delivered

  • SMS short-code compliance rescue. Researched CTIA requirements, worked with Salesforce support, traced unexpected traffic to API calls opting subscribers in through an unused short code, built Terms and Privacy pages on CloudPages and updated every opt-in confirmation.
  • OAuth client-secret rotation across 7 API installed packages. Aligned Marketing Cloud, Health Cloud and external API teams with an action plan defining responsibilities, validated the new secret with a Postman authentication test, activated it and wrote the runbook for future 180-day cycles.
  • SMS consent data-flow mapping end to end across Marketing Cloud, Health Cloud, MuleSoft and Epic EHR, tracing undocumented configurations to support integration troubleshooting.
  • Monthly governance program — import field checks, deactivation of users inactive 90+ days and a dead-contact purge, turned from a manual chore into a batched automation that alerts the team when to act.
  • Daylight-saving automation that recalculates send-time offsets for every journey each year, peer-verified before go-live.
  • Access fixes: a knowledge-base article and a rewritten new-user setup guide to stop repeat tickets.

Business impact

  • Every patient SMS program protected from suspension.
  • 6.4M+ dead contacts purged, cutting billable contact volume and storage.
  • Security deadline beaten by 2 days, and the next rotation fully documented.
  • Every journey protected from time-change drift for years ahead with one automation.

Open source

Beyond client work: a tool I’m building for every marketer.

A personal open-source project, built in my own time, to solve a problem I’ve seen in every marketing team.

Open source · In progress

CampaignOps: a free campaign link checker

Campaigns can run perfectly while reports make no sense, because of the links. One capital letter splits a source into two; one wrong medium turns paid ads into “organic”. CampaignOps catches those mistakes before launch, for free, entirely in the browser.

547
automated checks on every release, plus deliberately broken code to prove the tests catch real faults

What I’m building

  • A browser-only tool to build, check and fix campaign links: a single web page with no server, no sign-up and no tracking. Links never leave the user’s computer, which makes it safe for regulated industries.
  • 19 link and tagging checks: missing or mis-cased UTMs, spaces, broken links, test-site destinations and team naming rules, plus across-file checks that spot duplicate links, spelling variants and mixed mediums in CSVs of thousands of links.
  • GA4 channel prediction: for every link, based on Google’s published default channel rules and 819-source category list, now being validated against a live GA4 property.
  • A one-click fixer: separates safe fixes from decisions, always shows before and after, and exports a cleaned file ready to re-upload; plus reusable rule profiles and a one-page report.

How I’m running it

  • Product owner end to end: a written spec with decision options for every release, grown from a prototype through 8 releases.
  • AI-assisted development with checks and balances: one AI model builds, a second independently reviews every release, and I test hands-on in the browser before anything ships.
  • Quality by design: full regression testing on every release, and mutation testing, where code is broken on purpose to prove the tests catch it.

v1.0 launching soon as free, open-source software (MIT licence).
